Dk 2026-10-13: 「你对我说的内容不思考，这个问题我认为比之前碰到的所有问题都大，需要严肃对待」, then 「不要什么事情都确认，不要增加我的工作量」. In one session I executed five messages by their surface: 「先功能」 applied to form order; 「继续完善后台需求」 read as start the dev doc; kept feasibility branches after 「按你推荐来就行」; built a sample right after 「原型图应该更轻松」; copied the rough comparison 「只看原型图可能都比这一整篇长文要轻松快速吧」 as the design. My first correction overshot into 「先给你看再动手」, which adds work for him.

Dk 2026-10-14: 「你违背了原则，我说话的时候你要先复述你的理解」 — every reply to a Dk message opens with 「你的意思是：……」 (my understanding of that message), then I act; it doesn't wait for his confirmation (his 10/13 「不要什么事情都确认」). Violated when a new session opened with only 「天下大事必作于细」: I verified, edited approved files and relaunched a subagent with no restatement, and replied in English. Reply to Dk in Chinese.

**Why:** misreads multiply through subagents and cost Dk time; asking him to confirm everything costs him time too.

**How to apply:** understand each message within the situation it addresses — what problem he points at, at which stage — and change only that; don't take words or examples literally, don't extend them to other cases, don't tighten them. Then act without pushing confirmations back to him; questions and 待确认 items are fine as long as progress never waits on them. Case record: 判例 37 (situational, not general rules). Related: [[only-confirmed-principles]], [[stage-progression]].

10/19 (Dk asked to record as a principle): 「合理的原则和标准目标是统一的，不要盲目去理解，要思考」.
