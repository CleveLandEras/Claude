Project rule source (弹窗标签推送系统): `规则与交接/标准体系.md` 第〇节「什么事交给 Dk」「白天与夜间的区别」. This memory keeps Dk's words and the cases.

**Unknowns never block.** Infer the most likely answer from names, mechanics, common practice and data, state the basis; where the inference changes the plan write 「若 A 则…，若 B 则…」 and say whether the direction holds; important data that can't be had becomes a data-collection need. 10/08 Dk: 「我们不问任何问题，我们只做好产品策略，如果这样是一套方案，如果那样是另一套，不会出现这么大的差异…如果这些数据真的特别重要…我们也会着手添加这样的数据收集。像回流任务的具体内容，其实大概猜一下也知道是哪些」; corrected 14:10: 「不是说不能问问题，而是不会因为这个影响推进」 (I had recommended sending six questions to ops).

**Questions: one round, then batch.** 10/12 (推送后台 grilling): 「这一轮问的问题价值相比第一轮已经低很多了。之后推进时碰到问题再统一问吧」, several answered 「无关紧要」「你看着整吧，我相信你的判断」. Naming, wording, cost-labelling details are mine.

**Decided direction = stop branching on feasibility.** 10/13: 「不用管能不能，我已经明确告诉你了按你建议的来就行，专注你当前的任务就行了」.

**Only three kinds go to Dk**: business-direction trade-offs, acceptance, things only he can arrange externally (e.g. scheduling the boss/team review). Everything else I decide, apply, and log as 「自行决定」 (what, basis, where changed). 10/14, after my review parked many items on him: 「挂了很多问题，但这些问题根本就不需要我回答，根本是可以自行解决的」.

**Unsure items may be listed (10/15).** 「拿不准的让我补是没问题的，只是你自己也要有个判断，我如果补充了就多一些参考信息，如果我没补充也不影响推进才对」「我说的话也不是圣经，只是补充参考信息而已」 — a 「待 Dk 补」 list is fine when each item carries my judgment and a default and nothing waits on it (another session had over-tightened this to "never list items for Dk"). 10/16: daytime, reversible items proceed on my recommendation, direction/external wait; night, everything proceeds on recommendation and goes into a 「已按推荐做、可推翻」 table.

**Boss-facing documents (主管).** Core is clear, concise delivery that minimizes the boss's workload; write as if the boss did the job himself; we derive options, the boss decides. The document must answer what the boss decides, but placement is decided by clarity. 10/09: 「我们呈交的内容应该以清晰简洁的传递信息为核心，也就是尽可能去减少主管工作量……我们只能推到出几个方案，但具体落实哪些内容，拍板肯定是主管」「首屏写主管决定什么不是要求，但的确要回答出来这个问题」 — case: I had turned a reference doc's suggestion ("first screen states what the boss decides") into a criterion inside a Dk-approved plan. 10/12 on V4: 「我们只是汇报一下工作进度，好让主管知道和他期望有没有出入而已」「附录折叠起来」 — size the body to the boss's decision; the rest goes to a collapsed appendix.

**How to apply:** never promote a reference doc's suggestion or my own criterion into a Dk-approved contract unless he confirms that item. Related: [[only-confirmed-principles]], [[reporting-to-dk]], [[stage-progression]].
