Rule sources: 工作原则甲·1、甲·2 (the matching rows in 写作标准_合并版「协作与交付」 now point there; 10/16 现行说法), 标准体系第二节. Cases and Dk's words below.

**No waiting for 「开始」; drafts early (all projects, 10/16).** Push on from the checklist; for multi-step work give the plan and start right away; for a new project with no direction yet, give the plan in chat and start at the same time. Send a usable draft as soon as it exists; run review and polish in parallel; time-box subagent polishing. Dk 10/13 「不要什么事情都确认，不要增加我的工作量」; 10/14 「最大化效率肯定不是你我串行工作」; 10/14 after waiting ~1 hour while a subagent polished and review queued behind it: 「马上一个小时了，还没好吗」「有些事情是可以并行的吧？我是不是能提前先看呢？」; 10/15 「调整好立刻给我看，给我看和你的工作不冲突，不要等全部工作完成才给我看」. 10/16 Dk approved making this apply to every project (brief 现行原则与要求汇总, option 甲).

**Next stage only after a pass.** 「继续完善 X」 = improve X at its current stage. The next stage's deliverable needs Dk's 「通过」 or a context-free review / 判卷 pass he authorized (10/13 evening: 「整个原型图弄完你找个无上下文的agent审核吧……审核如果也通过就直接下一步」). Case 10/13 afternoon: told 「继续完善后台需求」, I started a developer doc; Dk: 「别直接跳到开发阶段啊，需求都还没明确吧，我没说通过呢啊」 (判例 35). A passed stage that a reviewer later flags still needs Dk or downstream evidence before reopening.

**Plan stage outputs in full, then go.** When Dk asks to plan stage outputs, deliver every output's TOC, what each section answers, tables, done-criteria, and where each of the boss's required deliverables lands — a list of stage names is not a plan. 10/08: 「我目前还是没看到完整的阶段性产出的规划…这个是当前最核心没有之一的先行内容，但是没有，这是流程缺失，而且我提过了这件事」. Send the plan to Dk to look at, but don't stop for his confirmation (10/08 「我主动来确认就行了，可以提醒我，但不要停止工作」; 10/13 「不要什么事情都确认」; changed 10/16 from "get it confirmed before executing").

**Validate one unit first (default).** If the method isn't proven (new format, repeated rejections), do the smallest unit first, check it, then roll out. 10/10: 「你先一页一页弄好，别一次性弄很多页，先一页能过标准再说」, and asked for the general rule (「这个是针对性的规则……要改成通用的」). Overrides: 10/10 「你改完别一页一页过了，直接全跑一遍，我完整的看吧」; 10/12 「确认，加章节分隔页，直接全跑一遍」. If Dk is away, keep going on the validated unit (「别暂停你的工作」).

**The unit check includes the project's own rules, not only reader comprehension (10/19 case).** A scheme taken from outside practice (e.g. Google design doc's "alternatives considered") gets checked against the project's confirmed rules before it becomes a template, and the sample gets the full pre-delivery checklist before rollout. I adopted a per-page "取舍 / 不采用" block and a fixed "要解决的问题" block for the push-backend review deck, checked the sample only by a reader-comprehension test, and approved it from a change log that literally said "取舍 2 条（均写不采用的理由）". The 写作标准 core row already said 「只写有效信息：没有过程痕迹……和被否决的选项」. Dk: 「不采用的取舍都写进评审材料里了？？？？……请说清楚为什么会这样，这个问题不解决不要继续推进了」. Also: when a handoff paraphrases Dk, quote his words and mark anything added (the handoff had turned 「不是展示设计」 into 「组成、关键设计、取舍」).

10/29 Dk: 「“不要什么都确认”不是一个清晰合理的规则」 — the 10/13 「不要什么事情都确认」 quoted above is no longer a basis; the two rules above stand on Dk's other quotes and his 10/16 approval.
