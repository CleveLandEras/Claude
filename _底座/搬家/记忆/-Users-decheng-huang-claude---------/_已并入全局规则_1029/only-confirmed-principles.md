When Dk criticizes one specific thing, fix that thing; do not promote it to a general principle (in memory, 工作原则, or progress logs) unless Dk stated or confirmed it as a rule.

**Why:** 2026-10-08 Dk, after I logged「页面上每个元素都要提供新信息」as a 原则 from his comment on a removed diagram: 「这不是什么原则，这个总结不成原则，你别瞎总结，确定的才能总结」.

Again 2026-10-09, about a task book an AI had written from his words: 「任务书不是我写的，是ai根据我的话写的，不是一手的标准」 — documents an AI derived from Dk's words (task books, principle texts, my summaries) are second-hand, even when he approved the surrounding work.

**How to apply:** Record Dk's own words as the rule; label my inferences as inferences; ask or wait before turning a one-off correction into a standing rule. When citing a standard, cite Dk's verbatim words; treat AI-written elaborations as non-binding unless he confirms that specific text.

Again 2026-10-12, after I recorded「数字必须带单位」from his remark that an unlabeled column of page numbers was unreadable: 「我没说数字必须带单位，很多事情要看情况，这个情况是不清晰……形成一个通用的解决方案不是数字必须带单位，是找到根因去用最保守的方案，多次出现再逐渐严格……这种瞎记录的问题需要审查一下有没有其他的胡写的不合理的限制」.
Method Dk set: (1) find the root cause of the complaint (here: the reader can't understand the element); (2) record the most conservative fix that addresses that cause, not a hard numeric/absolute rule; (3) tighten only when the same problem recurs. Hard thresholds (字数、字号、行数、个数) are at most defaults to guide, never rules, unless Dk stated them.

Recurrence 2026-10-13 (over-tightening): turned situational corrections into rules stricter than he meant (「矫枉过正」「你总收太紧也是问题」), then proposed recording his exact words as rules — also wrong: 「原话记为原则肯定也不合理，因为我的原话是针对某个情况的，不是通用的，你要注意的是不要把我的意思过度曲解」. Practice: record a correction as a case (situation, his words, the problem he meant, the fix) and apply it to similar situations only; no new general rule from one remark.
