Case (10/18, push-backend core five): after I relayed per-core progress, statuses, decisions and side notes, Dk said 「只专注于核心」「一定要参考顶尖优秀的做法」「整个全部做完之后，要向我汇报的点只有做了什么以及为什么做。其他的不用汇报」. Earlier the same day: 「预算示例重要吗？……完全没在重点上」, 「我没看见为什么改」.

**Why:** status, process, token, counts and peripheral tweaks bury the two things Dk reads for — the substantive change and its reason.

**How to apply:** when Dk sets a multi-part job like this, stay quiet until it is all done (notification turns: one short line at most), then report each change as 「做了什么——为什么」, core items only, no process or side notes. Every design point should cite how top products do it. Related: [[reporting-to-dk]], [[check-sufficiency-before-done]].
