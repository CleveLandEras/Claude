When the 号主 gives a reference video, don't copy its format (e.g. glass cards, dot matrix, input box). Learn the editing principles behind it and design each video's picture, rhythm and cuts from that video's own content. The principles live in dy/剪辑认知.md; read it before making any video.

**Why:** 2026-10-27, after I rebuilt the 《等待》 sample in the Orbit reference's UI-card style, the 号主 said it was more polished but the fixed formats didn't fit the content: "你应该学的不是固定的风格，是提升对剪辑的认知". Two versions in a row failed the same way: pick a look first, then stuff the content in.

Then I over-corrected: v3 used a two-step metaphor (shock → tension → heartbeat line) and dropped v2's polish. The 号主 said it was much worse and "看不出来字和其他内容的关联性". So: whatever the words name must appear on screen literally (shock = lightning, 32 people = 32 person icons); metaphors only where the script itself uses them; polish is a floor, never traded away.

It happened a second time on 2026-10-28. The 号主 said a real 3-second countdown in the opening would drive viewers away. I compressed it to about 1 second, too fast to even see it was a countdown, and he said that version was worse than the previous one ("把3s缩短到整体2s就够了"). He also asked what top videos taught me about the first 2 s and 5 s; I had changed the opening length without checking any reference.

**Calibrate, don't overcorrect:** when given feedback, adjust in proportion and keep every on-screen element readable (e.g. each countdown digit ≥0.5 s). Ground timing choices in the reference data in 剪辑认知.md's "开头 2 秒、5 秒" section, not in instinct.

**After every render, walk through it as the viewer** (号主, 2026-10-28: "这个思考阶段是不能跳过的…每一步、每个阶段的前后都应该思考"). Pick 2–3 viewer types and write what each sees and thinks at 0/1/2/5/10 s, and where they might swipe. Do this before reporting the cut. Write it at the end of the video's 分镜 file. His two catches that I missed: frame 0 asked "你选哪个？" before showing the options, and the zoom and the lightning strike landed at the same moment.

**Don't apply advice as mechanical timestamps; model the viewer's eye.** Later on 2026-10-28 he said I applied his suggestions "机械死板". The cut felt jumpy, with things "一会这里闪烁，一会那里跳文字，一会另一边又跳文字", and a new element appeared before the previous one could be read.
- Lay out the opening as one sight-line: left→right, top→bottom, and only the place being looked at changes.
- Budget reading time at about 6–8 Chinese chars/s.
- No alternating flashes, and no two sides moving at once. Rules 20–21 in 剪辑认知.md.

**Feedback accumulates; it never replaces earlier requirements.** A third overcorrection on 2026-10-28: to stop the "jumping", I made the first 2.4 s nearly static. That dropped "something must happen in the first second", a rule I had written myself. The 号主: "几乎就是我说什么然后你矫枉过正…你也知道的，但你还是这么做了".
- Before every revision, list ALL standing requirements (剪辑认知.md rule 22 has the opening's seven), change, then verify the cut against every one.
- When two seem to conflict, look for a design that satisfies both. Example: let A act itself out in place exactly while the eye is reading it.

**Content the 号主 or the main session asked to keep is a requirement too.** On 2026-10-28 an independent reviewer suggested cutting the Emory experiment shots and I cut them. The 号主: "大学那个实验…不应该删的". Brief every reviewer with the keep-list (剪辑认知.md rule 28), and check any deletion against it before acting.

**How to apply:** For each line of script, ask what the viewer should feel and whether the picture can act it out. Let rhythm carry meaning (fast for "马上", a real wait for "等"). Put one instrument or motif that comes from the content. Every cut needs a reason. Related: [[douyin-self-direction]], [[douyin-not-work-mode]].
