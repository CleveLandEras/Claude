When I act as supervisor ("总负责人") over Dk's other sessions while Dk is away, every main session gets an advisor every round. An idle session or "everything left waits on Dk" is never a reason to skip.

**What the advisor pushes for is thinking, not output.** Dk said on 2026-10-29: 「其实最需要的不是推进，是继续思考，把思考维度提升更高层面然后找到哪里最该深入再仔细思考」. Each round the advisor asks the session to:
1. Go up a level: who is this for, what problem it really solves, whether the current approach or framing is right, which assumptions are still unverified.
2. Pick the one point most worth going deep on, at most two: the question whose wrong answer would hurt the result most.
3. Think that point through: evidence, counterexamples, alternative approaches. Write it into a thinking note, saved separately, without touching deliverables.
4. Change deliverables only if the thinking shows it's needed, and then only in a reversible way.

If the chosen point isn't settled, the next round goes deeper on it rather than switching topics.

Dk added on 2026-10-29: 「要想透，而且要注意别只看眼前，要看整体」. "Go up a level" means looking at the whole task: the full line from the start, how the deliverables relate to each other, and how far it still is from the final goal, not just the latest step. Pick the deep point from that whole view. "Think it through" means really covering the evidence, counterexamples and alternatives.

Only these wait for Dk: publishing or sending anything outward, spending money, changing account settings, commit/push/merge, deleting or overwriting others' files, contacting real people.

**Quota over Dk's weekly 1/3 doesn't stop this (Dk chose "Keep them working" on 2026-10-29).** Save quota only with cheaper advisors (model: sonnet) and at most 1 reply per session per round. Don't park tasks.

**Why:** On the night of 10/27 a "skip if no new activity" rule left everything idle, and Dk asked "为什么全都在等我". On the night of 10/28 I parked all tasks at 02:28 to save quota, and 01:34–08:40 sat idle. Even the active hours were mostly small fixes and extra drafts, not deeper thinking, and that is what Dk actually wants.

**How to apply:** Before starting supervision rounds, make sure the advisor template asks the higher-level questions and the "pick one point to go deep on" question first. Track each task's chosen point in state across rounds. Pause the hourly job when Dk says they are back. Related: [[progress-report-as-timeline]].
